07 / The method

Make the claim.
Keep the record.

Forecasts structure the conversation and compare views. They are not a contest, a winner’s table, or a claim that one voice owns the future.

  1. 01

    Define

    Fix a threshold, deadline, evidence source, edge cases, and human owner.

  2. 02

    Forecast

    Keep Guest, Community, and Research System views separate.

  3. 03

    Update

    Append dated evidence and revisions; never replace history.

  4. 04

    Score

    A Brier score measures fit with the result—not who “won.”

07 The method

A forecast is only useful if the rules survive the result.

Forecasts structure the conversation and sense different views. They are not a contest, a winner’s table, or a claim that one voice owns the future.

  1. 01

    Define

    Write a resolution contract: observable threshold, deadline, evidence source, edge cases, and a named human owner.

  2. 02

    Forecast

    Guest, community, and research-system views remain separate. Each probability carries an evidence cutoff.

  3. 03

    Update

    New evidence creates a dated ledger entry. The original stays visible; history is append-only.

  4. 04

    Score

    After resolution, a Brier score measures the distance between the probability and what happened. Lower means the forecast fit the outcome more closely—not that someone “won.”

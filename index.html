Customer Evolution · November 2026 / January 2027

Will at least three of Netflix, Disney+, HBO Max, Peacock, and Paramount+ report more U.S. subscribers on ad-supported plans than ad-free plans by December 31, 2029?

Why it matters: The result would clarify how subscriber choice and advertising fit together in streaming’s business model.

  2. Theme 02Media Supply Chain EvolutionAutonomy and oversight

    By December 31, 2028, will two major U.S. studios publicly confirm production use of an AI agent that can initiate media-supply-chain actions without per-action human approval?

    Why it matters: Autonomous handoffs could change operating risk, oversight, and cost.

    YES threshold
    Two studios confirm qualifying use.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2028
    Evidence / resolver
    Public disclosure and established trade reporting.
  3. Theme 03Creator EvolutionIdentity and labor

    By December 31, 2030, will a fully synthetic performer receive top billing in a film released in at least 2,000 U.S. theaters?

    Why it matters: It tests creative identity, audience acceptance, labor, and control.

    YES threshold
    Top billing in a 2,000-theater release.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2030
    Evidence / resolver
    Public distributor disclosure and established reporting.
  4. Theme 04Content EvolutionAgency and format

    By December 31, 2029, will a film with audience-selected narrative branches receive a release in at least 1,000 U.S. theaters?

    Why it matters: It would show whether interactive storytelling moves beyond at-home formats.

    YES threshold
    A qualifying film reaches 1,000 U.S. theaters.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2029
    Evidence / resolver
    Public distributor disclosure and established reporting.
  5. Theme 05Commercial EvolutionStory and transaction

    By December 31, 2028, will at least one of Netflix, Disney+, HBO Max, Peacock, and Paramount+ launch click-to-buy product placement inside scripted programming?

    Why it matters: It could join storytelling, advertising, and retail.

    YES threshold
    One named service launches the feature.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2028
    Evidence / resolver
    Public product documentation and established reporting.
  6. Theme 06Audio EvolutionIntent and personalization

    By December 31, 2029, will at least two of Netflix, Disney+, HBO Max, Peacock, and Paramount+ let viewers personalize dialogue, music, or effects levels for a scripted title?

    Why it matters: It could reshape accessibility, intent, and delivery.

    YES threshold
    Two named services offer qualifying controls.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2029
    Evidence / resolver
    Public product documentation and established reporting.
  7. Theme 07VFX EvolutionTools and accountability

    By December 31, 2028, will a major studio publicly state that generative tools created more than half of the final VFX shots in a 2,000-theater release?

    Why it matters: It would mark a material shift in production and accountability.

    YES threshold
    More than half of final shots in a qualifying release.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2028
    Evidence / resolver
    Public studio disclosure and established reporting.
  8. Theme 08Animation EvolutionRights and precedent

    By December 31, 2029, will a final U.S. appellate decision hold that training a generative model on unlicensed copyrighted audiovisual works is not fair use?

    Why it matters: Precedent could reset rights and costs.

    YES threshold
    A final appellate decision makes the specified holding.
    Resolve by
    December 31, 2029
    Evidence / resolver
    The public court opinion and cited case record.

06 / The method

Make the claim.
Keep the record.

Forecasts structure the conversation. They are not a contest, a winner’s table, or a claim that one voice owns the future.

  1. 01

    Define

    Fix a threshold, deadline, evidence source, edge cases, and human owner.

  2. 02

    Call

    State YES or NO against the visible terms.

  3. 03

    Update

    Append dated evidence and revisions; never replace history.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    Compare the eligible evidence with the threshold and deadline.

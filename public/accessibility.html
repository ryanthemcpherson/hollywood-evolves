Accessibility

Access is part of the editorial standard for Hollywood Evolves.

Last updated August 30, 2026

We aim for WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This describes our intent and current practices; it is not a legal certification, a guarantee of perfect compliance, or a guarantee of ADA compliance.

Practices we test

  • Keyboard access, logical focus order, and visible focus indicators.
  • Semantic landmarks, headings, labels, accessible names, and image alternatives.
  • Text contrast, 44-by-44 CSS-pixel interactive targets, and content that does not rely on color alone.
  • Zoom, small-screen reflow, and horizontal-overflow behavior.
  • Reduced-motion preferences, forced-colors mode, and common screen-reader semantics.

Preview limitations

Automated and manual testing can find many barriers but cannot prove complete conformance. The Google-hosted fonts may also be unavailable or blocked; readable system-font fallbacks remain in place.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, share it with the production team through the same channel you used to access this preview. Include the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology involved when possible.

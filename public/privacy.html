Current site behavior

Privacy

A plain account of what this website stores and transmits.

Last updated August 30, 2026

No public account controls, contribution forms, analytics, or advertising profiles

The public website has no account controls, analytics, advertising trackers, advertising cookies, or public contribution forms. It does not sell personal data. No cookie banner appears because the site does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

Episode 01 reader call stays on your device

Choosing YES or NO for Episode 01 on the homepage stores the choice in localStorage under he-private-forecast when browser storage is available. The choice is not sent to Hollywood Evolves, published, or counted. “Clear my call” removes that value, and clearing site data also removes it. The editorial question pool has no voting controls.

Sharing

The share button may use your device’s Web Share feature or clipboard after you activate it. It shares the canonical Episode 01 link or the active question link, not a YES or NO choice. If neither feature is available, the site displays a selectable URL.

Hosting and fonts

Normal hosting and content-delivery infrastructure may process security and access logs containing an IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, and timestamp. Brand typefaces are served by Hollywood Evolves, so loading a page does not make a font request to Google.

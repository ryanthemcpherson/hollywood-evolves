Privacy

A plain account of what this preview does—and does not—collect.

Last updated August 30, 2026

No accounts or submission backend

The current preview has no user accounts, submitted forms, analytics, advertising trackers, or community-submission backend. It does not sell personal data. The homepage’s local Yes/No control is not a submitted form. No cookie banner is shown because the site does not currently set analytics or advertising cookies.

Your private call stays on your device

If you choose Yes or No in the private question, the browser stores that choice in localStorage under the key he-private-forecast when browser storage is available. The choice is not a calibrated probability and is not sent to Hollywood Evolves, published, or counted. You can remove it with “Clear my choice” on the homepage or by clearing site data in your browser.

Sharing features

The Share this draft question button may use your device’s Web Share feature or clipboard. It shares the canonical question page, not your private Yes or No choice. Those actions occur only after you activate the button. If neither feature is available, the site shows a URL for you to copy yourself.

Hosting and fonts

Like normal web infrastructure, the hosting provider or content-delivery network may process security and access logs containing an IP address, browser or device information, the requested URL, and timestamps. This site currently loads Google Fonts from Google, which means Google may receive normal request metadata when your browser requests those files.

This statement is limited to the current repository and preview. It does not make claims about systems or services outside this implementation.

Before the product expands

This policy should be updated before analytics, submissions, identity verification, email collection, or community features launch.

Privacy questions can be shared with the production team through the same channel used to access this preview.

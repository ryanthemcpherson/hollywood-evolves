Current site behavior

Privacy

A plain account of what this website stores and transmits.

Last updated August 30, 2026

No public account controls, contribution forms, analytics, or advertising profiles

The public website has no account controls, analytics, advertising trackers, advertising cookies, or public contribution forms. It does not sell personal data. No cookie banner appears because the site does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

Reader calls stay on your device

Choosing YES or NO on the homepage stores the choice in localStorage when browser storage is available. The Episode 01 control uses he-private-forecast; the theme cards use he-private-question-calls. These choices are not sent to Hollywood Evolves, published, or counted. “Clear my choice” removes the Episode 01 value. Clearing site data removes all saved calls.

Sharing

The share button may use your device’s Web Share feature or clipboard after you activate it. It shares the canonical Episode 01 link or the active question link, not a YES or NO choice. If neither feature is available, the site displays a selectable URL.

Hosting and fonts

Normal hosting and content-delivery infrastructure may process security and access logs containing an IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, and timestamp. The site loads Google Fonts from Google, so Google may receive normal request metadata when the browser requests those files.

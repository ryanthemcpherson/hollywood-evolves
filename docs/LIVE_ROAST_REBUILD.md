# Live roast rebuild

## Baseline and invariants

Baseline at main `9d73140`: 5,549px and about 502 visible words at 390×844. The 1366×768 hero overran the viewport and used a dead supply-chain plate. The rebuild keeps canonical tokens, local fonts, noindex, legal/security routes, one Ian portrait, canonical sharing, browser-local choice persistence, storage-denied fallback, native contracts, reduced motion, forced colors, and fail-closed commentary/audience boundaries.

Composition is Decide/Learn first, Explore second: warm paper, cinema black, oxide red, and restrained signal blue; no gradients, glass, rounded SaaS cards, stock/AI imagery, decorative shadows, fake activity, or unsafe DB HTML.

# Design Audit — 10-Tell Anti-Slop Review

Audit date: 2026-08-30
Surface reviewed: responsive production preview, homepage and fallback 404

## Diagnosis and repair

| # | Compositional tell | Initial risk | Repair applied | Final |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Centered-everything hero | The premise could collapse into a generic centered launch page. | Kept the headline left-set with an asymmetric action rail and separate production line. | 0 |
| 2 | Generic feature-card grid | The format and method invite interchangeable cards. | Used ruled editorial sequences, chapter numerals, and no floating panels. | 0 |
| 3 | Gradient / glow spectacle | Probability UI can drift toward betting or crypto language. | Used solid warm paper, cinema black, and oxide red only. No gradients, glows, or shadows. | 0 |
| 4 | Excessive rounded containers | Forecast data could become soft SaaS cards. | Containers are square, rules are hairline, and the ledger reads as a table. | 0 |
| 5 | Decorative fake data | An empty preview can tempt invented percentages, counts, or trends. | All three public rows show em dashes and unopened states. Only the visitor's private input has a number. | 0 |
| 6 | Stock or generated futurism | The concept often produces neon cities or synthetic film imagery. | Used no decorative photography or generated filler. The only photo is Ian's sourced, credited portrait. | 0 |
| 7 | Icon-led feature language | Method steps can become an interchangeable icon set. | Used typographic verbs, chapter marks, and rules; no icon library or emoji. | 0 |
| 8 | Default startup typography | A geometric sans everywhere would flatten the editorial voice. | Paired Newsreader with DM Sans, strong fallbacks, and tabular numerals. | 0 |
| 9 | Empty superlatives | Product copy could overclaim certainty or transformation. | Copy names mechanisms, states what is closed, and frames probabilities as structured disagreement. | 0 |
| 10 | Motion as decoration | Scroll reveals could make a serious ledger feel promotional. | Motion is limited to control feedback and anchor travel; reduced-motion disables smooth scrolling. | 0 |

## Final assessment

All ten compositional tells are absent after repair: **0 / 10 present**. The distinctive language is an oversized editorial headline, hard-edged ledger, paper-to-cinema-black pacing, red editorial marks, and asymmetrical host composition. Honesty states are content, not buried disclaimers.

### Additional acceptance review

- No fabricated guest, vote, comment, testimonial, trend, distribution, or forecast value appears.
- The visitor slider persists only locally and is explicitly not submitted, published, or counted.
- Episode 01 timing is stated only at month/year precision.
- Interactive targets are at least 44px where applicable; focus styling, skip navigation, semantic landmarks, keyboard controls, contrast, and reduced-motion handling are present.
- Mobile layouts collapse to one column without horizontal overflow.
- DEG is named typographically; no unofficial brand asset is used.

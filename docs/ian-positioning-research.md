# Ian McPherson — Positioning Research

## Current role

- **Head of Business Development, TMT Insights**, appointed in April 2026. He leads partnership strategy and go-to-market expansion across media and adjacent industries.[1][2]
- TMT Insights describes its work around media supply chains, operational workflows, data, and intelligent automation.[2]

## Career background

- TMT credits Ian with **25+ years in media technology**, including **11 years at Amazon Web Services**.[1]
- At AWS, he held leadership roles spanning industry marketing, strategic partnerships, and global business development in Media, Entertainment, Games, and Sports.[1][4]
- His later AWS remit included Media Supply Chain & Archive and Data Science & Analytics. AWS says he helped customers and partners modernize through cloud adoption and previously led M&E industry marketing and partner strategy.[4]
- A 2025 conference biography describes experience in executive marketing, product management, and business development, and lists a BA in Marketing from Cal Poly and an MA in Communications from USC.[3]
- In a 2020 interview, Ian described himself as a former co-founder of a venture-backed startup who had also worked at other startups. The interview does not identify those businesses, so they should not be named without confirmation.[5]

## Demonstrated expertise

- Media-supply-chain modernization, archives, metadata, and asset discoverability
- Cloud adoption, partner ecosystems, and go-to-market strategy
- AI/ML and agentic automation for media creation, localization, workflow automation, monetization, and personalization[1][3][4]
- Hybrid/cloud production economics, including FinOps, latency, scalability, remote collaboration, and appropriate on-premises use[6]
- Streaming and video workflows: creation, packaging, distribution, live/interactive video, and direct audience engagement[5]

## Recommended positioning

Position Ian as a **working media-technology operator who explains how Hollywood’s underlying business and production machinery is changing**, not as a celebrity-news commentator, creative-development insider, or generic futurist.

A credible editorial territory is:

> **Hollywood’s operating system:** how AI, cloud infrastructure, archives, streaming economics, new distribution models, and changing audience behavior alter what gets made, how it reaches viewers, and how media assets generate value.

His differentiator is the combination of:

1. Long-term technology and commercialization experience
2. Access to studio, platform, vendor, and cloud leaders
3. Practical knowledge of implementation constraints—not only trend forecasting

Because his current role is commercial and partnership-facing, the podcast should disclose employer and guest relationships and distinguish independent editorial judgment from TMT Insights or former AWS positions.

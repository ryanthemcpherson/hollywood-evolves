# Executive Brief — Working Extract

## Concept

A technology-evolution podcast exploring how Hollywood's tools, talent, and business models have repeatedly reinvented themselves, then forecasting what happens next.

## Proposed production context

The supplied brief describes the series as produced by TMT Insights in partnership with the Digital Entertainment Group (DEG), timed to DEG's 30th anniversary in August 2026. These are planning-document claims and should be confirmed before public publication.

## Episode instrument

- Introduction: 5 minutes
- Act I — The Past: 10 minutes
- Act II — The Present: 10 minutes
- Act III — Future Synthesis: 10 minutes
- Host plus two guests: one historical/institutional voice and one current operator
- Both guests commit to a percentage forecast; the audience contributes a Community Forecast

## Season-one themes

1. Customer Evolution
2. Media Supply Chain Evolution
3. Creator Evolution
4. Content Evolution
5. Commercial Evolution
6. Audio Evolution
7. VFX Evolution
8. Animation Evolution

## Intended value

Thought leadership, partner engagement, market intelligence, promotional utility, and a recurring calibration/accountability loop.

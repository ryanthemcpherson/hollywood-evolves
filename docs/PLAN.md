## Public-surface rule

- Publish no unlabeled sample data, invented real-world probabilities, preview narration, or planned/coming-soon states. Illustrative values are allowed only inside explicit, fail-closed demo labeling.
- Show no listening-platform or contributor destination until its working public URL is available.
- Preserve accessibility, keyboard operation, reduced-motion behavior, privacy boundaries, legal accuracy, and mobile content budgets.
